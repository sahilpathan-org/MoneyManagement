Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Paisa ("Paisa," "we," "the app") is a private, browser-based personal money management application. This Privacy Policy explains how Paisa handles information.

Information stored by the app

Paisa stores the following information locally in your browser using local storage:

  • Expense amounts, dates, categories, payment methods, and notes
  • Daily, monthly, and yearly budgets
  • Your display name
  • Your language and light/dark theme preferences

This information is kept in the browser profile on your device. Paisa does not send, upload, sell, or share these records with a server or third party.

Browser storage

Browser local storage is not encrypted storage. Anyone with physical or software access to an unlocked browser profile may be able to view stored information. Clearing browser data or deleting Paisa's local data removes the app records.

Local storage may be lost if you clear site data, reset or replace your browser profile, reinstall your browser, or otherwise remove the app's data. You should keep exported backups in a secure location.

Exports and downloads

Paisa lets you export expenses and budgets as a JSON backup or generate PDF reports. These files are created on your device and are not sent by the app to any server.

Exported files contain personal financial information. They may remain in your Downloads folder or other storage location, and they may be copied or shared by you. Please keep and share them securely.

Internet services

Paisa loads Chart.js and jsPDF from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts. Loading these resources can cause your browser to contact those providers and disclose standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser information.

Paisa itself has no backend, account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, or in-app payment system. Your expense records are not sent to Google Fonts or jsDelivr.

Permissions

Paisa does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or the phone network. Any operating system or browser permissions shown outside the app are under the control of your browser or device.

Children's privacy

Paisa is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because records remain on the user's device, parents or guardians should supervise use and protect exported files.

Your choices

You can:

  • Change or delete individual expense records in the app
  • Change or remove your saved budgets and display name
  • Export a JSON backup
  • Restore a previously exported JSON backup
  • Use Delete everything in Settings to remove stored app data
  • Clear Paisa site data in your browser to remove all remaining app storage

Data security

Paisa uses local device storage rather than remote servers, but no application or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a lock screen, keep software updated, and avoid storing exported reports on shared or public devices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Paisa's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will show the latest revision. Continued use of Paisa after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the contact details provided on the Paisa app distribution or download page from which you obtained the app.
